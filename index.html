FIELD NOTES

Draw in order. Rectangles, ellipses and circles use a two-click corner-to-corner workflow. Polygons use centre, then radius. Arcs use start, through, end.

Snap like a surveyor. Point snapping grabs endpoints, corners, centres, midpoints and edges from every visible, unlocked layer. Hold SHIFT to constrain to ortho while drawing.

Presets are groups. Click any piece to move the whole landmark. Hold ALT to edit one wall or prop. Use CTRL + G on arbitrary selections to group them.

Exports. Blueprint PNG includes a title block. SVG is clean vector line work. JSON preserves the full editable document.
